## How to Review This Document

We've listed the standard task types and workflows based on typical CA practice operations. Please:
- **Confirm** which ones are needed for V1

## 1. GST Compliance

### Monthly Tasks (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B)

| Step | Description | Typical Role | Days Before Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect sales and purchase data from client | ARTICLE | -10 |
| 2 | Reconcile GSTR-2B with purchase register | JUNIOR_CA | -8 |
| 3 | Prepare GSTR-1 data (outward supplies) | JUNIOR_CA | -7 |
| 4 | Prepare GSTR-3B computation (liability + ITC) | JUNIOR_CA | -5 |
| 5 | Manager review of returns | MANAGER | -3 |
| 6 | Partner approval and filing | PARTNER | -1 |
| 7 | Save filed acknowledgement and challan | JUNIOR_CA | 0 |

**Questions:**
- Is this the standard workflow your firm follows?
- Any steps missing (e.g., client confirmation before filing)?
- Do you do GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B as one combined workflow or separate?

### Quarterly Tasks (GSTR-1 Quarterly for QRMP)

### Annual Return (GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C)
| Step | Description | Typical Role |
